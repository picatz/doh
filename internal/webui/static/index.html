      Account & passkeys

      Add a passkey

      Sign in with your face, fingerprint, or device PIN: nothing to type, and nothing anyone can phish. Once anyone has one, sign-in links from doh ui need a passkey to finish.

      Appearance

      People

      Who can sign in to manage this network, each with their own passkeys.

      Alerts

      Hear about threats and trouble as they happen: as notifications in this browser, by email, on your phone with the free ntfy app, or anywhere a webhook goes.

      Service accounts & API

      For scripts, home automation, and CI. Each acts for one tenant, as a reader or an admin, with keys that always expire. Rotate a key to replace it without downtime; revoke one to stop it at once.

      Tenants

      Share this server with other households. Each tenant manages its own profiles, devices, query log, and people, and sees no one else's. Its profiles select devices by DoH path or host name, or by address within the networks you give it.
